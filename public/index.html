BUSINESS MEETS COMMUNITY

A useful connection
can change what
happens next.

Spare boxes. Surplus stock. Furniture with years left in it. What one business no longer needs could be exactly what another is looking for.

Exeter Circular Business Network is an independent nonprofit student initiative making those connections easier. We bring local businesses together around a practical idea: keep useful resources circulating in our city.

Membership is completely free and will always be free. Our aim is to reduce unnecessary purchasing and waste while building a stronger local business community.

WHAT CAN CIRCULATE?

Start with what’s
already going spare.

Our first focus is simple, usable resources that businesses can exchange locally.

Packaging & boxes

Clean shipping boxes, reusable packing materials and surplus protective packaging.

Furniture & fittings

Usable desks, chairs, shelves and display fittings ready for another workspace.

Materials & offcuts

Usable untreated timber, fabric remnants and suitable materials for makers.

Examples of potential exchanges. Members check condition and suitability and agree collection directly. Food, chemicals, hazardous materials and electrical equipment are outside our initial scope.

A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Before you join.

Simple, local and built
around real participation.

Who can join?

Businesses based in Exeter or serving the local area that want to offer or find usable resources. Independent shops, offices, makers and other local enterprises are welcome to enquire.

Does membership cost anything?

No. Membership is completely free and will always be free. We are a nonprofit student initiative, with no joining fees or ongoing subscriptions. Businesses agree any payment for individual items directly with each other.

How do members communicate?

Members use dedicated communication channels to share offers, post requests and arrange exchanges. We review membership enquiries and share access privately once your business is confirmed. You do not need a separate website account.

What will our membership certificate say?

Your dated certificate will show your business name and state that you are part of the Exeter Circular Business Network for Sustainability. Members can display it alongside a window sticker to show their involvement in the local network.

Who arranges collection?

The businesses involved agree condition, suitability, any price and collection directly. The network helps make the introduction; it does not provide storage or a delivery service.

Is this run by the University or council?

Exeter Circular Business Network is an independent, student-led nonprofit initiative. University or council endorsement is not implied.

How are my details used?

Membership enquiries are used to discuss participation. We ask permission before publishing your business logo, member profile or photographs. Before joining member communication channels, review their privacy information and choose the business contact details you are comfortable sharing with other participants.
